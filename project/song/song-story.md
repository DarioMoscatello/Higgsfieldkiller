# Song story — "Mirror Mirror"

## What this note is

- It describes one song for a reader who cannot hear it: what the words are
  about and how the music moves from start to end.
- It covers the whole song, which is 148.8 seconds long. The passage used for the
  video is the first 79.2 seconds, and one part of this note says where that
  passage sits.
- It describes the song only. It says nothing about pictures.
- Nobody listened to the song to write this. Everything comes from an automatic
  analysis and an automatic transcription of the voice. Loudness and silence are
  measured; every other value is an estimate or a model's suggestion.
- The words themselves are not written out here. Lines are pointed to by
  their start time (`lyric-lines.md`).
- Title and artist are known only from the file name: "Mirror Mirror - Anno
  Domini Beats".

**Times.** A position is written m:ss.s, counted from the first sound of the
song. A length is written in seconds. The passage starts at 0:00.0, so the
passage clock and the song clock are the same. The analysis files count from
0.025 seconds earlier, so their raw times are 0.025 seconds later than the times
here.

## The song in a few sentences

The speaker talks to a mirror and asks it who will rise and who will fall. He
says he has been avoiding his own faults and is still standing proud. He tells
the mirror not to lie, and says his own eyes show it to him; the line does not
say what "it" is.

Between those returns to the mirror he looks at the world around him: a mother's
warning not to trade who you are for approval, people with status who lost it
quickly, people who traded their standing for money, and being a single error
away from ruin. Later he turns to people wanting display more than depth, to
pain made presentable, and to what he values now compared with before.

The attitude is self-examination. He does not claim to be perfect. He sets it
down as he lived it, and adapts even if the rules are unfair.

## A few terms

- **Beat:** the steady pulse you would tap your foot to.
- **Tempo:** how fast the beats come. Here about 75 beats a minute, so one beat
  every 0.8 seconds.
- **Bar:** a group of four beats, 3.2 seconds here. The voice mostly delivers
  one line per bar.
- **Hook:** the part whose words come back unchanged several times.
- **Verse:** a part with new words each time.
- **Layer:** one strand of the sound that the analysis separated out: voice,
  drums, bass, and "other instruments".
- **Drum hits:** the analysis sorts them into low hits (a deep thud), sharp hits
  (a snare or clap) and light hits (quick ticks).
- **dB (decibel):** the unit for a difference in level. About 6 dB is a doubling
  of signal strength; a rise of 10 dB is usually heard as about twice as loud.
- **Key and chord:** a key is the set of notes a song is built on; a chord is
  several notes sounding together.

## The passage the video covers

- **Where it sits.** It is the first 79.172 seconds of the song, 0:00.0–1:19.2,
  as one continuous piece with nothing removed. This was checked by matching the
  trimmed file against the full song in five-second blocks. The trimmed file is
  about 0.9 dB quieter than the full song throughout, with no fade at either end.
- **What is in it.** The hook once, the whole of verse 1, then the hook twice,
  with its last line cut short. It holds the first big jump in level (0:25.6),
  the break before the hook returns (0:51.0–0:54.4) and the loudest part of the
  passage (0:54.4 onward).
- **How it begins.** With the first word of the song, on the hook, without
  drums. Nothing comes before it.
- **How it ends.** The cut at 1:19.2 falls in the middle of the last line, not
  at its end. The word timings put the cut inside the sixth of that line's ten
  words. The last three words start about 0.4 seconds or more after the cut and
  are not in the passage; the seventh starts about 0.1 seconds after the cut and
  is almost certainly not in it either. In the other four repeats the same line
  runs to the next bar line, which here is 1:20.0, so about 0.8 seconds of the
  line is missing. Word times are estimates.
- **The last beat.** The fourth beat of the final bar is placed at 1:19.18, which
  is 0.008 seconds after the cut. That is closer than the analysis can time a
  beat (its stated margin is 0.07 seconds), and it finds a drum hit at the same
  spot, so the data cannot say whether the very start of that hit is inside the
  passage.
- **What comes right after it in the full song.** The bar finishes at 1:20.0,
  and there the level drops into a quiet part. Verse 2's first line is stamped
  at about 1:19.6, overlapping the end of the hook line, so its exact start is
  uncertain. The passage stops about 0.8 seconds before the drop, at full level.
